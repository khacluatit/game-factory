Pixel Mines — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Pixel Mines ("the App") is made by BeeMob Technology and Services Company Limited. This policy explains how the App handles information.

We do not collect data

The App has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device.

Saving pictures

When you choose to save or share a finished picture, the App opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded by the App.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
