Quiet Pagoda (Chùa Tĩnh Tâm / 靜心寺) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Quiet Pagoda ("the App") is made by BeeMob Technology and Services Company Limited. This policy explains how the App handles information.

We do not collect data

The App has no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Your progress, settings and the private wishes you write are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. No server is involved.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Simulated characters

The pilgrims shown in the temple are simulated characters generated by the App. They are not other users.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
