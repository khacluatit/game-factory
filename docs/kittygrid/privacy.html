Kitty Grid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This policy explains how the mobile game Kitty Grid ("the App") handles information.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email or contacts. Game progress (scores, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. You can decline tracking in the system prompt or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
