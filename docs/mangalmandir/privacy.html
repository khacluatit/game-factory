Mangal Mandir (मंगल मंदिर) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Mangal Mandir ("the App") is made by BeeMob Technology and Services Company Limited. This policy explains how the App handles information.

We do not collect data

The App has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Your progress, settings and the city you choose for the Panchang are stored only on your device.

Location

The App does not use location services. Sunrise times are calculated from the city you pick in the App.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. No server is involved.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple and unlock decorations inside the App only. No offering is made at any real temple. We never receive your payment details.

Simulated characters

The devotees shown in the temple are simulated characters generated by the App. They are not other users.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
