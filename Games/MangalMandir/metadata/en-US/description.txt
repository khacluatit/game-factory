A peaceful Hindu temple in your pocket. Walk in through the torana gate, leave your footwear at the rack, ring the ghanta and stand before Shri Ganesha for darshan.

DAILY AARTI
Light the aarti thali and draw circles clockwise to wave the lamp before the deity, with manjira and temple bells keeping the rhythm. After seven rounds, draw the flame toward you to receive the aarti and your prasad.

FIVE SHRINES, A DEITY FOR EACH DAY
• Shri Ganesha in the main sanctum, under a Nagara shikhara
• Lord Shiva as the linga, with Nandi facing him
• Mother Lakshmi on her lotus, with elephants on either side
• Shri Hanuman with his gada
• Shri Krishna with his flute
Monday opens at Shiva, Tuesday and Saturday at Hanuman, Wednesday at Ganesha, Thursday at Krishna, Friday at Lakshmi. On Sunday, offer water to Surya in the courtyard.

PUJA, THE RIGHT WAY
• Arpan: offer modak and durva to Ganesha, bael leaves to Shiva, sindoor and ladoo to Hanuman, tulsi and makhan to Krishna, lotus and kheer to Lakshmi. Only the right offerings appear for each deity.
• Abhishek: pour water or milk over the Shiva linga.
• Parikrama: walk clockwise around the sanctum, or a half circle around the Shiva linga.
• Japa: count 108 beads on sandalwood, rudraksha or tulsi mala. The mala turns back at the guru bead.
• Path: read the Hanuman Chalisa, Gayatri Mantra, Mahamrityunjaya Mantra and the aarti of each deity in Devanagari, with transliteration and meaning.
• Water the tulsi and offer arghya to the rising sun.
