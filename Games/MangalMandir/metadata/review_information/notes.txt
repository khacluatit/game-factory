1. Purpose & audience: Mangal Mandir is a calm, offline 3D devotional app (4+) set in a Hindu temple. Users ring the temple bell, perform aarti by drawing circles with a finger, offer flowers and food items to the deity, pour water over the Shiva linga (abhishek), count japa beads, read traditional prayers (Hanuman Chalisa, Gayatri Mantra, aartis) and see a Panchang calendar with local reminders. The devotees shown are simulated characters.
2. How to use: Launch the app, read the 3 onboarding cards and tap Begin. Tap "Enter the temple" to walk in from the gate (tap to skip). In the temple, use the arrows to change shrines, tap the hanging bell, and use Aarti, Offer, Japa and Read. The courtyard button leads to the tulsi plant and the sun offering. Settings and Decorations are at the top right. No account or login is needed.
3. External services: Apple StoreKit only. In-app purchases are 5 non-consumable decorations: Deepmala lights, Marigold decor, and three japa malas. Restore Purchases is in Settings and Decorations. There are no ads, servers, analytics, accounts or AI. Local notifications are scheduled on device. No location services are used; the user picks a city for sunrise times.
4. Regional differences: none, except that the UI language follows the device (English or Hindi).
5. Religious content: prayers are traditional public-domain texts (Rig Veda, Tulsidas, Samarth Ramdas and traditional aartis). Purchases are decorations only, not donations, offerings or services at a real temple; the app states this in Settings and the store.
6. All 3D models, art and sounds are original and generated in code.
