The classic tile-sweeping logic game, where every tile is a pixel of a hidden picture.

Open the safe tiles, flag the ink bombs, and watch the picture appear piece by piece. Finish the board and the whole artwork lights up and goes into your album.

NEVER GUESS
Every board can be solved with pure logic from the very first tap. No more 50/50 coin flips.

FROM SNACKS TO MASTERPIECES
• 60 hand-made pixel pictures in 5 chapters
• Pictures grow rarer as you go: Common, Rare, Precious and Legendary
• Chapter 4: world wonders, from the Pyramids to the Statue of Liberty
• Chapter 5: legendary paintings, from The Starry Night to Mona Lisa
• A new daily picture with streak rewards

RELAXED AND FAIR
• No timers, no energy, no waiting
• Hint lamp and shield when you get stuck
• Hold a tile to flag, tap a number to open around it
• Save finished pictures to Photos
• No ads, no account, no data collected, plays offline
