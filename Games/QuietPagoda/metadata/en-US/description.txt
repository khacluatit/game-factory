A small Vietnamese pagoda in your pocket. Step into a candle-lit 3D hall, sit on a cushion before the Buddha and take a few quiet minutes for yourself.

• Bow, count prayer beads, strike the wooden moktak, or simply sit with palms together
• Chant along with the Heart Sutra, the Universal Gate verses and the verse of repentance, line by line to the beat of the wooden fish
• Finish 108 bows or 108 beads and a lotus blooms in the pond outside
• Light incense once a day for free
• The hall follows your clock: soft dawn light, golden afternoons, lanterns at night
• Morning and evening chanting times, plus full-moon and new-moon days from the lunar calendar
• Gentle reminders you can turn on or off
• No chat, no accounts, no ads. Everything stays on your phone and works offline

The pilgrims who come and go in the temple are simulated characters, so the hall never feels empty. They are not real people, and no one can see you.

Optional offerings ($0.99 each) such as agarwood incense, a floating lotus lantern or a fruit tray stay in the temple for 24 hours. You can also keep robes, sandalwood beads and Lunar New Year decorations forever.
