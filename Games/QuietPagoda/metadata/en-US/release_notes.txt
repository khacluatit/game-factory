What's new in 1.3:
• The temple is rebuilt in northern Vietnamese style: a three-door gate with a bell loft, a stone path with balustrades, a Guanyin island in the lotus pond, a second gate, a red-brick courtyard and a main hall with curved tile roofs
• New Mother Goddess shrine, Patriarchs' hall and stupa garden, each with its own incense
• The main hall now has the Three Buddhas, the Nine Dragons, guardian deities, the Ten Kings and couplets
• See the whole temple from above, follow a 10-stop route or wander freely, with a temple map
• Lunar New Year: the Tet pole, New Year couplets, lanterns, fireworks on New Year's Eve and a spring blessing to pick each day from the 1st to the 15th of the first lunar month
• Pilgrims in the temple are simulated characters
