1. Screen recording (physical iPhone, latest iOS): see the link in our reply in App Review / Resolution Center if requested.
2. Purpose & audience: Quiet Pagoda is a calm, offline 3D meditation app (4+) set in a Vietnamese Buddhist pagoda. Users bow, chant with prayer beads and a wooden fish, light incense, keep a daily streak and journal, and receive lunar-calendar reminders (new and full moon). The other pilgrims shown are simulated characters, and the app discloses this on first launch.
3. How to use: Launch the app, read the onboarding and tap Start. Tap a cushion to sit, then use Bow, Gassho, Beads or Mõ. Tap the incense burner to light free incense or view offerings. Tap the bell to ring it. The journal and settings are at the top right. No account or login is needed.
4. External services: Apple StoreKit only. In-app purchases are 3 consumables (incense, lotus lantern, fruit offering, each lasting 24 hours in the temple) and 3 non-consumables (robes, sandalwood beads, Lunar New Year decor), each $0.99. Restore Purchases is in the Journal. There are no ads, servers, analytics, accounts or AI. Local notifications are scheduled on device.
5. Regional differences: none, except that the UI language follows the device (English, Vietnamese, Traditional Chinese).
6. Regulated industry / third-party material: not applicable. Purchases are virtual cosmetic offerings, not donations or charity. All 3D models, art and sounds are original and generated in code.
